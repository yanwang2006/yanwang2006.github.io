A closer look. A broader perspective.

Understanding energy.
Engineering possibility.

We study how thermal and mechanical processes shape the way energy moves, transforms, and can be put to work.

Led by Dr. Yan Wang, our group combines theoretical and computational approaches to investigate transport across length and time scales—with a focus on energy applications and advanced manufacturing.
