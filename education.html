01 / Teaching

Heat Transfer Classes

ME314 introduces students to the various processes of heat transfer. ME414/614 covers classical approaches to solving complex heat transfer problems. ME714 delves into advanced theories and modeling techniques for addressing heat transfer challenges.

K-12 Outreach

We are committed to educating and motivating K-12 students to pursue higher education and careers in STEM fields. We offer lab tours and science demonstrations during events such as Engineer's Day, Nevada Bound, and other programs for local K-12 students.

03 / Senior design

Capstone Projects

Dr. Wang has mentored several Capstone design groups to design thermal energy conversion devices through science and engineering principles. Past projects include solar-thermal converters, solar-thermoelectric converters, and thermal interface materials.
